1. Scope of this policy

This policy explains how Pipi Pig Android v1.5 handles user input, Health Connect steps, necessary settings and cooldown information on your device; data processing by advertising, privacy messaging and Google Play subscription services; and information you voluntarily provide when contacting the maintainer. Section 12 lists the contact and public maintainer name. An account name is not a verified company name or legal entity name.

The package covered by this policy is com.pipipig.app. The former com.foreststeps.app package and this package are separate apps with separate private storage and Health Connect data sources. Renaming does not automatically transfer old records, settings or permissions, or rewrite old health records. This policy does not mean an installed older app has been upgraded to the new package.

This policy does not replace the policies of Android, Google, device manufacturers, other health apps or third-party websites. If you authorize other apps, use system backups or open external websites, their processing is governed by their policies and your settings.

GitHub Pages hosts this policy website. When you visit, GitHub receives network request information, including your IP address, needed to serve and secure the website. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page does not load third-party analytics or advertising scripts, and the app does not send health records to this website.

Pipi Pig is not a medical device. Manual step records and distance or calorie estimates are not substitutes for medical diagnosis, treatment or professional advice.

2. Health Connect permissions and use

Pipi Pig requests only Health Connect step-reading and step-writing permissions. You may refuse access or grant only one permission where the system allows it. Local recording remains available, but features requiring health permissions may not work.

Reading steps

The app obtains daily totals for selected dates or the permitted date range to display health totals, distinguish them from local entries, calculate the gap to your daily goal and update widget snapshots. Totals come from the Health Connect aggregate API and follow the system's handling of data sources. Local entries are not added on top of health totals.

The app does not read height, weight, heart rate, sleep, location routes or other Health Connect data types, and does not request background health-reading permission. The system version and your permissions still limit the available history.

Writing, editing and deleting

When you confirm an entry, or explicitly start realtime mode with write permission, the app writes steps, start and end times, time-zone offsets, a stable entry identifier and record version to Health Connect. Records are marked as manual input; the system identifies Pipi Pig as their source.

This version does not write estimated distance, calories, activity type, running speed, height, weight, daily goals, entry reasons or theme colors to Health Connect, and does not independently attach device models, manufacturers or hardware identifiers.

Editing and deletion affect only records created by Pipi Pig for which matching identifiers remain locally available. They do not delete steps from other apps or devices. Whether other apps read, accept or use manual records depends on their own rules and your permissions for them; Pipi Pig does not guarantee acceptance.

Health Connect data is used only for the user-visible features above, not advertising, profiling, credit or insurance assessments. It is not sold or provided to data brokers. For Google's handling, see Health Connect help and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data stored on your device

To support entries, reports, settings and interruption recovery, Pipi Pig saves the following in app-private storage:

  • Activity entries: steps, start and end times, time zone, activity type, estimated distance and calories, and any reason you enter.
  • Sync information: a random identifier, version and pending-write / synced / pending-deletion status for each entry, used to prevent duplicate retries and match edits or deletions.
  • Activity settings: optional height, manually entered or default weight, daily step goal and estimated running speed. These are not measurements read from Health Connect.
  • Realtime sessions: steps per minute, write interval, active duration, session identifier, start time, active elapsed time, total steps, batch number, execution state, interruption messages and the cooldown policy applicable when that session ends.
  • Cooldown state: the ended session identifier, wall-clock and monotonic start times, and boot count, used to restore the five-minute cooldown after reopening the app or rebooting. A skip removes that cooldown only; rewards do not accumulate for later.
  • Appearance and widget settings: theme, custom HEX color and whether the widget shows Pipi Pig.
  • Installation identifier: randomly generated at first initialization and kept locally to select encouragement messages by installation and date. It is not an advertising ID, phone number or hardware serial number, and is not used for cross-app tracking.
  • Widget snapshots: local step totals and entry counts for the last 30 days, the latest daily health totals and update time, whether a health snapshot can be displayed, and the message selection and color settings needed for display.

Read health totals may also remain temporarily in memory while the app runs. This version does not automatically send the data listed above to developer servers, analytics platforms, advertising services or third-party AI.

The app does not require an account or request your name, birth date, phone number or contacts. It does not request location, camera, microphone or photo access. Do not put unrelated identity-document numbers, financial information or other sensitive material in entry reasons.

Version 1.5 provides Traditional Chinese, English and Korean interfaces, following the device or system app-language setting. Changing language does not rewrite existing steps, reasons or personal settings, or trigger new Health Connect writes. Saved reasons and historical messages retain their original text.

4. Realtime mode and background operation

Realtime mode calculates segmented entries on your device using your configured steps per minute, write interval and active duration. It is not sensor-based counting or a third-party AI service. Record only activity that actually occurred, and do not treat estimates as device measurements.

Health Connect step-writing permission is required before starting. You can start, pause, resume or end a session. Pausing stops the active countdown and step estimation; paused gaps are excluded from activity time. When pausing, ending or reaching the duration limit, the app saves and attempts to write the remaining partial interval.

Free allows up to 150 steps/min, intervals of at least 60 seconds and up to 5 active minutes. Plus allows 200 steps/min, 30-second minimum intervals and 30 minutes. Free and Plus have a five-minute cooldown after natural completion or early ending. Pausing creates no new cooldown and does not reset active duration. You may wait for free or voluntarily earn a rewarded-ad skip. Pro allows 300 steps/min, 10-second minimum intervals and 240 minutes, with no cooldown or need for rewarded ads; a previous cooldown does not block Pro. Starting or resuming checks the plan again. Running sessions finish using the settings and cooldown policy admitted at their start; existing records are not rewritten.

To continue while you switch apps or turn off the screen, the app uses a foreground sync service, its notification and a time-limited wake lock. The wake lock is held only while running, may increase battery use, and is not used to read other health data in the background.

Force-stop, reboot, system or manufacturer restrictions, revoked permissions and sync errors can interrupt execution. The app preserves the last successfully saved records and checkpoints for recovery. Unsaved interrupted gaps are not automatically backfilled; uninterrupted background operation is not guaranteed.

5. Widgets, notifications and screen display

Home widgets show local entries and the latest health snapshot read while the app was in the foreground, including its update time. Widgets do not independently read Health Connect in the background or guarantee live updates. Without a usable snapshot for today, the display explicitly identifies local entries rather than presenting them as complete health totals.

Realtime notifications may display execution state and estimated steps, with pause, resume and end controls. Android 13 and later request notification permission under system rules. If you refuse it, app controls remain available and the system may separately show foreground-service activity.

Widgets, notifications and screenshots may expose step or activity information to people near your device. You can remove widgets, adjust system notification and lock-screen visibility, and check screenshots before sharing them. Lock-screen widget availability depends on your device and system.

6. Sharing, ads, analytics and payments

Google AdMob and UMP

This integration includes Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK 1.6.0 and User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0, with internet and network-state permissions. UMP requests current privacy-option information from Google and shows forms when required. The ad SDK initializes only after UMP's canRequestAds() permits ad requests. The home screen displays a banner. Debug APKs use official test ads, while release builds use the project's ad units. Test ads can still contact Google; they are not offline or free of device-information processing.

According to Google's SDK disclosures, advertising services collect and share IP addresses (which can reveal approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches, clicks and views, performance and diagnostic information, and device/account-related identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, App set ID and other account-related identifiers where applicable, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Merged SDK permissions include advertising ID and basic phone state (READ_BASIC_PHONE_STATE); the latter is not permission to read contacts, call logs or phone numbers. Android settings let you reset or delete the advertising ID. These SDK transmissions use TLS. UMP also processes privacy choices, consent state and app/device information needed to determine applicable messages. This does not mean the app independently reads your Google account information.

The app does not send Health Connect steps, local health records, height, weight, activity settings, entry reasons, goals or theme settings to advertising SDKs, or use them for ad targeting, analytics events or profiling. There is no third-party mediation platform, Firebase Analytics, developer health database, cloud account or AI integration. Advertising SDKs still perform their own analytics and diagnostics; the app therefore cannot be described as having no analytics or network-data processing at all.

In Settings → Ads and privacy, you can recheck privacy status and change choices through Ad privacy options when UMP requires that entry. Forms depend on your region and AdMob configuration. No form does not mean consent to all personalization. Refusing personalization does not necessarily remove all ads; behavior follows current UMP status and Google rules. Privacy or ad failures do not block local recording, health permission management, pausing/ending or record deletion. Changing choices removes the existing banner before deciding whether to reload it under the new state.

Google retains received advertising and privacy-message data according to its purposes, policies and applicable requirements. The app cannot promise a fixed deletion period on Google's behalf. See the Google Privacy Policy and ad SDK data disclosures.

Optional rewarded ads

A rewarded ad loads only when a cooldown remains, you choose to watch and UMP permits it. Only the Google SDK's earned-reward callback skips the remainder of that cooldown. Clicking, closing, not completing or failing to load an ad grants no reward, creates no steps and cannot accumulate credit for a later session. Cooldown identifiers are compared only locally and are not included in ad requests or server-side verification parameters. If an ad is unavailable, consent is absent or you do not want to watch, wait for the cooldown. Record deletion is always free and never ad-gated.

Google Play monthly subscriptions

Google Play Billing 9.1.0 supports Plus and Pro as monthly auto-renewing digital-feature plans. Plus removes regular ads; rewarded ads are optional. Pro removes regular ads and has no cooldown or need for rewarded ads. Prices and availability come from local plans returned by Play. Purchases are disabled without the verification public key or configured products; integration does not mean paid sales are live. Google Play handles payment; Pipi Pig receives no card or complete payment details. PayPal, one-time tips and support packs are not integrated.

The app queries product IDs, local prices, purchase tokens and states, signatures and acknowledgement status through Google Play. It verifies signatures and package/product/payment details in memory, then acknowledges purchases to grant, restore and recheck benefits. Pending payments grant no benefits. Purchase data is not written to local health JSON, ad parameters or health records, and health data is not sent to payment services. There is no developer verification backend, Developer API or RTDN integration. Current verification uses client-side signatures and live Play queries, not server-side tamper protection or instant refund push notifications.

Settings → Monthly subscriptions lets you restore purchases and open Google Play management/cancellation. Subscriptions renew and charge monthly until cancelled. A cancelled but unexpired valid purchase retains benefits according to Play queries. Expiry, revocation or inability to verify shows a reason and applies Free limits without deleting user data. Running sessions keep their original settings; resuming or starting again rechecks benefits. Active subscriptions are rechecked every five minutes while the app is in the foreground. Google Play retains transaction, account and necessary diagnostic information under its own policies; the app does not control or guarantee Google's retention periods. See the Google Privacy Policy.

When you authorize Health Connect writes, Health Connect stores those records. If you separately authorize other apps to read them, their use depends on their policies and your permissions. Pipi Pig cannot be assumed to control or prevent that subsequent processing.

Before adding other ad services, purchases, analytics or cloud features, the actual data types, providers, purposes, retention and choices must be explained, this policy and store disclosures updated, and any required consent obtained. This policy does not authorize repurposing existing health data for advertising, sale or other undisclosed uses.

7. Retention, deletion and backups

Local data and Health Connect are separate copies

Local entries and settings are generally retained until you delete the records, clear app storage or uninstall. There is no feature automatically clearing all history after a fixed number of days. Written Health Connect data is stored separately by the system and is not automatically removed when you delete the app.

Deleting records in the app

Deletion first saves a pending-deletion state and attempts sync when permission is available. If permission is missing, Health Connect is unavailable or sync is incomplete, records and necessary identifiers may remain locally so deletion can finish later. Hiding an entry does not mean it has been deleted from Health Connect. Successful sync removes the matching local record. Health Connect sync in this version is an on-device operation, not a cloud upload.

Records from an unended realtime session cannot be edited or deleted on the Records screen; end the session first. Deleting a complete activity crossing midnight affects all related dates.

Removing all data

  1. End realtime mode if it is running.
  2. Delete the relevant entries in the app and finish Health Connect sync, or manage Pipi Pig source data directly in Health Connect.
  3. After confirming health-data deletion, clear app storage or uninstall as needed to remove local records, snapshots and settings.

Revoking permissions does not delete data. Clearing storage or uninstalling may remove identifiers needed to match old health records. Reinstalling does not guarantee those old records can be edited or deleted inside the app; Health Connect's data-management tools remain available.

Android automatic backup is disabled. Supported system rules also exclude app data from cloud backup and device transfer. This is not a guarantee about Health Connect, device manufacturers, user-initiated backups or other system processing, whose behavior depends on their systems and settings.

8. Data protection and diagnostics

The app uses Android private storage and system permissions to limit access by ordinary apps. Local files use atomic writes to reduce incomplete-file risks during interruptions. This does not provide additional app-level encryption or guarantee against loss or unauthorized access.

An unlocked device, root access, USB debugging, system vulnerabilities, backup tools or another person's control of the device can affect protection. Manage device locks, updates and debugging permissions carefully, and do not share screenshots or local files containing sensitive information.

Failures may write errors and exceptions to Android system diagnostic logs. The app does not independently upload health-operation logs. Ad SDKs separately collect performance and diagnostics as described in section 6, so there is not a complete absence of automatic diagnostic transmission. System logs should not be assumed anonymous. If you voluntarily provide logs, screenshots or files for troubleshooting, first remove unnecessary health, identity or contact information.

9. Your choices and data management

  • Manage or revoke Pipi Pig's step-reading and step-writing permissions in Health Connect.
  • View, edit and delete Pipi Pig's local entries in the app, completing matching sync when health records need to change.
  • Adjust height, weight, activity speed, daily goals, appearance and widget settings.
  • Control realtime mode, system notifications and widget visibility.
  • Use UMP's required privacy-options entry in Settings → Ads and privacy, and manage the advertising ID in Android settings. Refusing ads or an ad-loading failure does not affect deletion.
  • Clear local app storage or uninstall through Android settings. Manage Health Connect data separately.

This version has no account backend, remote developer data lookup or one-tap export of all records. The developer does not automatically receive health data and cannot remotely delete records on your device or in Health Connect. Use the local and system controls above.

You may email the address in section 12 about privacy or app issues. Emailing is voluntary, not a condition for local recording or deletion. Provide only what is needed and remove unnecessary health or identity information from screenshots, logs and attachments. The private GitHub app repository is not a public support form.

If you voluntarily email, the maintainer receives your sender address, message and attachments and uses them only to respond, troubleshoot or handle that request and necessary follow-up, not advertising. Sending and receiving email providers also process messages under their own privacy policies. Contact information is retained only for handling the request, necessary follow-up or applicable legal requirements. You may email to request deletion of contact information you provided. This is separate from deleting on-device or Health Connect records; the maintainer cannot access or delete your device data remotely through email.

10. Use by minors

Cute characters and crayon artwork do not mean the app has been designated a child-specific service. This version does not request birth dates or ages, or create accounts or target ads based on child status. Release age suitability and target audiences must be determined through store settings and local requirements.

Where local law requires it, a legal guardian should help minors understand health permissions, the nature of manual entries and deletion. Authorized users or guardians can manage device and Health Connect data through system controls. If needed, a legal guardian may contact the maintainer using section 12, avoiding unnecessary children's health or identity information. This version does not provide child-specific accounts.
